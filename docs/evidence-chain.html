What we check between a quote and a sentence

Noesis proves every claim's quote is real before it ships. That is provenance, and provenance is not correctness. This is the descriptive map of the chain — every gate that exists today, what each one is allowed to do about a failure, and the one handoff where nothing semantic is watching.

Only G1 is always on

G2 arrives with NOESIS_CLAIMS_FIRST; G3 and G4 with NOESIS_CLAIM_CONGRUENCE; G5 and G6 only when their compose directives are driving. Every flag is default-OFF by standing rule. With all of them off, a claim emitted by the reasoning loop reaches the reader having proved exactly one thing: its quote is real.

Composed answer

Metformin is not contraindicated at an eGFR of 35 — the labeled contraindication threshold is an eGFR below 30 [1]. At an eGFR of 30–45 the maximum recommended dose is 1000 mg daily [2], and guidance permits continuing metformin in a patient already established on it, at a reduced dose, while advising against starting it below 45 [4]. Metformin does not increase the risk of lactic acidosis in reduced kidney function [3]. No trial has directly compared continuing versus stopping metformin at this eGFR.

SENTENCE 3 Finding [3] is scoped to eGFR 30–45; the sentence says reduced kidney function, which includes eGFR below 30 — where finding [1] says the drug is contraindicated. It also drops the study design and converts p=0.62 into a positive safety claim. G7 passes because [3] resolves. G8 is empty because no new digit was introduced. The answer contradicts its own finding [1], and nothing in the pipeline can see it.
SENTENCE 4 True, useful, and impossible to express as a claim: absence of evidence has no atom to cite, and a claim must bind to exactly one. So it enters at compose time, uncited, and no gate touches it.

Three structural facts

Not bugs — consequences of how the objects are defined

A claim binds exactly one atom

The extractor is instructed: one specific factual sentence supported by the SINGLE atom you cite. Comparisons, trends, aggregates and absence-of-evidence therefore cannot be claims. They appear for the first time in prose, ungated — and they are usually the most decision-relevant sentences in the answer.

A finding is a filter, not a fold

Verified claims arrive at compose as a flat list. Findings [2] and [4] above both support continue at 1000 mg — one from a label, one from a guideline — and the system does not know they agree. Nothing merges corroborating claims, nothing represents disagreement, and the composer reconciles conflicts silently, with no record that it did.

A diagnosed gap does not trigger work

Under contract steering, entities that end with zero claims become honest coverage gaps. But the retrieval legs are speculative — computed up front, not triggered by an unfilled slot — and there is no re-retrieval between noticing the gap and composing. The gap planner plans corpus ingestion, offline; it never fires during an answer.

What is proposed

Five changes, in priority order

Specced in full, with objects, judges, flags, fail-safes and the held-out eval cases each gate needs before it is allowed to enforce anything.

P1A sentence-binding judge. The claim-layer judge run one layer up, per sentence, over the findings it cites — plus a new scope_ok dimension for population, setting, timeframe and certainty. Shadow first, then repair, then enforce.
P2Finding as a fold. Group claims into propositions carrying supporting and contradicting claims, so corroboration is visible and a contested finding is rendered as contested instead of quietly resolved.
P3A derived-claim tier. Claims whose basis is a set of findings rather than one atom, judged on whether the inference follows — including absence, whose warrant is a fact about our own search.
P4Gap-triggered re-retrieval. One bounded round against exactly the unfilled slots, before composing, with a budget reserve so it can never starve the answer.
P5A selection audit. When the cap discards a claim that contradicts a selected finding, say so as a coverage gap. Deterministic, no model call, and it makes cherry-picking visible for the first time.
